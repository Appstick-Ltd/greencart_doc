# Hana Go Documentation

Welcome to the official **Hana Go** documentation! Here you will find guide details, app architecture, and API references for the Hana Go grocery e-commerce app.

Hana Go is a premium grocery e-commerce mobile application designed to connect users with fresh farm produce, pantry essentials, and household items.

---

## App Screens & Features

Explore the user flow and design structure based on our official UI layout:

### 1. Home Screen
The main portal for users, featuring:
- **Location Selector**: Let users set their delivery address easily.
- **Top Deals Banner**: Visual promotions like **"Up to 40% Off Fresh Groceries"** to boost conversions.
- **Dynamic Categories**: Quick access to *Vegetables*, *Fruits*, *Dairy*, *Pantry*, *Snacks*, *Beverages*, *Frozen*, and *Household*.
- **Limited Time Offers**: Promoted products with time-sensitive deals.
- **Recommendations**: Personalized food items and grocery suggestions based on user shopping history.

### 2. Browse & Search Screen
A dedicated search and filter panel allowing customers to:
- Browse structured sub-categories (e.g., *Daily Purchases* like Onions, Garlic, Ginger, Coriander).
- View item grids for vegetables (e.g., Cabbage, Potato, Herbs, Tomato, Capsicum, Cauliflower).
- Filter items by price, discount, and availability.

### 3. Shopping Cart & Checkout
Allows users to review items, adjust quantities, apply coupon codes, and select their preferred delivery slot.

### 4. Recipes & Meal Prep
Integrates smart grocery shopping with meal preparation by suggesting recipes and letting users add all needed ingredients directly to their shopping cart in one tap.

---

## Key Modules

- **Location & Delivery API**: Manages geo-coordinates and estimates delivery times for nearby hubs.
- **Product Catalog Management**: Handles inventory levels, category structures, and discount tags.
- **Order Processing**: Secures payments and triggers delivery driver assignments.
